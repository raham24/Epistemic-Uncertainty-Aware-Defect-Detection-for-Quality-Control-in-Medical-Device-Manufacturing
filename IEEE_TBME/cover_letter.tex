    \opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

    We are pleased to submit our manuscript \emph{\customtitle} for consideration as a Full Paper in
    \textit{IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering}.

    This work introduces a principled rejection strategy for improving
    the reliability of machine-learning-based defect detection in medical
    device manufacturing. We leverage epistemic uncertainty to identify
    predictions on which the model should abstain. Our theoretical analysis
    establishes the validity of the approach and extends previous analysis on the regimes under which it is effective. Experiments on
    synthetic benchmarks and real-world medical device report data show
    that the strategy enables explicit control of predictive coverage while
    improving performance on the retained samples.

    The significance of this work lies in its potential to support safer
    and more reliable quality assurance in medical device manufacturing.
    By flagging uncertain cases for additional inspection, the proposed
    approach can reduce the risks and costs associated with incorrect
    automated decisions.
